# Question

One item of a [[Quiz]], tagged with [[Notion]]s. Types: single-choice, multiple-choice, scenario / trade-off, and short free answer. The first three are graded locally; free answers are graded by the LLM through a [[Generation]].

## Related

- Belongs to a [[Quiz]]
- Tagged with [[Notion]]s
- Answers are recorded as [[Attempt]]s

## In code

Tables `questions` and `question_notions`, repository `src/main/db/repositories/assessment.ts`. See [[Data Model]].
